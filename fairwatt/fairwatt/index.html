FairWatt
Prototype · Synthetic data

Know what your electricity delivers, and what it really costs.

FairWatt measures the service each neighbourhood receives, compares it with what households pay, and shows regulators and utilities what a fairer tariff would cost to fund.

All figures are synthetic and built from public sector data: Band A at ₦225 per kWh, bands B to E subsidised, about 36% distribution losses and 81% collection efficiency (NERC). Generator cost is assumed at ₦600 per kWh. Replace with distribution company and feeder data when a partner is available.
